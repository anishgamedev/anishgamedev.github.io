About Indiandevanish

Namaste! 🙏 I'm Anish — on the internet, Indiandevanish. I'm an indie game developer from Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, building games that feel like home: our roads, our festivals, our chai breaks.

My games

My main game is Indian Auto Wala Rush — you drive an auto-rickshaw through busy Indian streets, pick up passengers, and survive baraats, red signals, cricket kids and one very naughty monkey. It runs in the browser and there's an Android APK on itch.io. More games are coming.

Why this site?

I wanted one place where friends (and anyone searching) can play my games instantly — plus a collection of good, family-friendly free games from partner networks, so there's always something to play. Partner games are made by other studios and are shown here through the official embeds from GameMonetize and GameDistribution; they may show ads, which helps keep this site free.
