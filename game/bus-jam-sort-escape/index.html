About the game

Clear the traffic in Bus Jam - Sort Escape, a colorful single-player puzzle with 1,000 levels. Tap available buses to pick up passengers of the same color, manage limited road spaces, and merge three matching buses into a double-decker. Plan each move, use Clear, Shuffle and Magnet boosters when needed, and unlock pixel-art collections as you progress. Play with a mouse or touch controls. Progress is saved in the current browser.

Controls

Desktop Left-click buses and buttons Mobile Tap buses and buttons Send buses to collect passengers of the same color keep road spaces available and combine three matching buses Use the on-screen boosters when you need help
