About the game

The Goal: Build four HomeCells by suit in ascending order (Ace to King). The Rules: Move: Shift the bottom card of any column to a FreeCell to uncover the cards beneath. Build: Move cards between columns in descending order with alternating colors (e.g., a Red 9 on a Black 10). Empty Spaces: Any card can fill an empty tableau column—use these spots wisely to maneuver! The Win: Keep moving cards one by one until all suits are stacked in their HomeCells.
