Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use anishgamedev.github.io ("the site"), run by Anish (Indiandevanish).

What the site itself collects

Nothing directly. The site is a set of static pages: there are no accounts, no sign-up forms and no comment boxes. The site's own pages do not set cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when pages are requested (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Third-party games and advertising

Most games on the site are loaded from third-party game networks — GameMonetize and GameDistribution — inside an embedded frame. These games show advertisements (for example before a game starts or between levels). The game networks and their advertising partners, which can include Google, may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where allowed, personalise ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Revenue from these ads is shared with the site owner.

This happens under the third parties' own privacy policies:

  • GameMonetize — gamemonetize.com/privacypolicy
  • GameDistribution — GameDistribution end-user privacy notice
  • How Google uses data from sites that use its services — policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Games may also save your progress in your browser's local storage. Indian Auto Wala Rush saves your coins and settings only on your own device.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; games still work, ads may be less relevant.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads from Google at adssettings.google.com and from many networks at youronlinechoices.eu or optout.aboutads.info.
  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the EU/UK), the game networks show their own consent message before personalised ads.

Children

We pick family-friendly games, and the site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ad networks are responsible for serving ads in line with child-privacy laws for the games they deliver. Parents: if you have a concern, please contact us.

Links to other sites

The site links to other websites (itch.io, social share buttons, partner networks). Their privacy practices are their own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
